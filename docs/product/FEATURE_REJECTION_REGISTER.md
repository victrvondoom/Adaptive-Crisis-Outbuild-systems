# Feature Rejection Register

Candidates rejected after evaluation. Not part of the accepted portfolio.

## REJ-001 — Cryptocurrency donations
Do not add unrelated financial instruments to a humanitarian logistics platform.

## REJ-002 — Social feed feature
Decorative social features add no operational value.

## REJ-003 — Public leaderboard of NGOs
Gamifying aid delivery risks harmful competition.

## REJ-004 — Automated beneficiary eligibility decisions by AI
AI must never autonomously deny essential assistance.

## REJ-005 — Bulk SMS to real beneficiaries without consent
Requires explicit consent and authorization; not implemented.

## REJ-006 — Fake weather forecast generation
Forecast fabrication is prohibited; only real sourced data.

## REJ-007 — AI confidence score widget without evaluation dataset
Unexplained confidence scores are deceptive.

## REJ-008 — Second optimizer duplicating the authoritative one
Two sources of execution order violate the P0 invariant.

## REJ-009 — Unauthenticated public dashboard of tenant data
Tenant isolation is a hard boundary.

## REJ-010 — Beneficiary facial recognition
Unlawful biometric processing; rejected.

## REJ-011 — Auto-posting mission data to social media
Privacy risk without beneficiary consent.

## REJ-012 — Hardcoded demo KPIs
Fabricated metrics are prohibited.

## REJ-013 — Agent-written audit events without business postconditions
Agent text is never success evidence.
